What to copy from ServiceNow
  1. In ServiceNow, open My Weekly Time Sheet and navigate to the week you want to log.
  2. Scroll down to the Logged Time Cards table — make sure all your task rows are visible.
  3. Select the table (starting from date on the upper left e.g.March 23 - March 29, 2026 to the last entry on bottom right) and copy (Ctrl+C).
  4. Paste here. The parser reads the date range header and every task row with non-zero hours.
Installing the Addanex extension
  1. Click ↓ Addanex extension above and unzip the downloaded file.
  2. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
  3. Enable Developer mode (toggle in the top-right corner).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  5. Done — the button appears automatically when you open the Addanex portal.
